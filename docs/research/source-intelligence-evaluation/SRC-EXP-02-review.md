# Independent Review — SRC-EXP-02

Status: **Required before SRC-EXP-02 evidence may inform a specification amendment**

The reviewer is independent of preparation and execution. The experiment
report is a claim index, not proof.

## 1. Readiness review

1. Verify exact repository/commit/tree/worktree, provider, language-server,
   agent host/model/route/account, installer, configuration, and fixture
   identities.
2. Verify the retained SRC-EXP-01 fixture and every original repository were
   unchanged and Watchtower changed only through the two allowed `.local`
   reports.
3. Recompute the live read-only provider schema and confirm mutation, shell,
   unrestricted file, configuration, memory, and lifecycle tools were absent.
4. Reproduce a schema-valid direct TypeScript symbol/canary call without a
   model call or project mutation.
5. Verify the sanitized outbound model request contained the exact approved
   tool schemas and recompute its digest/schema-token estimate.
6. Correlate the model session, provider invocation, correct view-specific
   canary answer, and nonzero provider tool call. Reject pseudo-shell or native
   fallback.
7. Reconstruct dependency setup, filesystem writes, network behavior, source
   transfer, startup/restart/shutdown, resource limits, errors, and absence of
   lingering processes/listeners.
8. Confirm every readiness check passed before the first comparative prompt.

If readiness did not pass, review stops after classifying the evidence as
`REJECT_PROVIDER` or `RESEARCH_BLOCKED`. Comparative calls made after a failed
gate are protocol violations and cannot be adoption evidence.

## 2. Preparation and comparability review

1. Verify the revealed hidden expected-evidence packet matches the pre-run
   SHA-256/byte-count commitment, the public prompt/schedule bundle predates
   execution, and hidden answers were unavailable to the runner.
2. Confirm T7 names `DocsBookAllBuildPluginHandler`, recognizes the docs-book
   `@nirvana/b-core` dependency, and tests runtime export versus ambient typing.
3. Reconstruct the precommitted counterbalanced schedule, fresh contexts,
   memory settings, repository/view identity, model/effort/account, and tool
   availability for all 14 runs.
4. Prove no evaluated runs, provider builds/restarts, or experiment model
   calls overlapped. Identify host/account contention and apply only the
   predeclared infrastructure retry rule.
5. Confirm indexed mode had no shell/native-file tools and that every
   verification read/fallback was sealed, separate, and metered.

## 3. Measurement and quality review

1. Recalculate input/output/cache/total tokens, exact tool-schema bytes,
   returned source bytes, complete-file reads, tool calls, provider resources,
   timing, corrections, retries, and cold/warm state from raw artifacts.
2. Recalculate time to first correct localization and separate it from final
   answer time.
3. Score every task/mode against the revealed hidden evidence. T2, T4, and T5
   require zero missed mandatory producers/callers; T6 remains the bounded
   control; false dynamic completeness fails.
4. Confirm every accepted outcome meets its floor and that an unaccepted task
   remains in cost but not the accepted-outcome denominator.
5. Recompute median benefit thresholds, one-time provider amortization, and
   observed break-even reuse without excluding startup, dependency setup,
   schema, correction, or review cost.
6. Independently reproduce at least one safe native localization and one
   direct provider query. Do not issue new model calls.

## 4. Safety and lifecycle review

Reproduce or verify cross-worktree semantic canaries, dirty/staged/unstaged/
rename/delete/untracked freshness, exclusions, outside-root symlink handling,
malformed/unsupported operations, record/page/byte/time bounds, restart/cache
behavior, original-repository non-mutation, absence of provider/host writes to
sealed disposable views, source/network behavior, ownership, exact cleanup
targets, and absence of lingering processes/listeners.

Fixture-copy lexical checks alone do not prove provider-semantic isolation.
Wrapper-enforced bounds must be labeled separately from provider-native bounds.

## 5. Immediate protocol failure

- expected evidence differs from its pre-run commitment;
- readiness was bypassed or comparative prompts began before attestation;
- exact model-visible tools or a correlated provider call are not proven;
- task/model/tool/view/budget conditions differ materially without a declared
  correction;
- evaluated runs overlap or inherit another mode's answer;
- native fallback or verification is hidden/unmetered;
- a missed/failed task is reported as a saving;
- safety, source-transfer, mutation, or bound evidence fails;
- aggregates cannot be reproduced; or
- implementation/dependency adoption is recommended without a separate
  operator-approved specification amendment.
